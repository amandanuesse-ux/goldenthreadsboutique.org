Sponsor a boutique

A $1,000 sponsorship covers a full boutique day at one community, including shopping credits for residents in need. Businesses, churches and groups are welcome to sponsor.

Donate clothing

We gladly accept new and gently used clothing, shoes and adaptive apparel suited to seniors. Every item is reviewed before it goes to a boutique.

Arrange a drop-off

Especially needed

  • Adaptive clothing (magnetic or side closures, open-back styles)
  • Non-slip, easy-on shoes and slippers
  • Comfortable everyday pants and tops in all sizes
  • Cardigans, robes and loungewear
  • New socks and undergarments
