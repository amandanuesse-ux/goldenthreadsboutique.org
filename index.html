Dignity, comfort and choice

Every senior deserves clothing that fits their body, their needs and their sense of self. Shopping credits mean no resident is left out because of cost.

Golden Threads Boutique is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving senior communities in St. Charles County, Chesterfield and the greater St. Louis area.

How a boutique visit works

We handle the setup, the shopping and the cleanup. Your team just picks a date and lets residents know we're coming.

  1. Pick a date

    Request a visit and we'll plan a time that fits your activity calendar.

  2. We set up the shop

    Racks, mirrors and a private try-on area, stocked in a full range of sizes.

  3. Residents shop

    Volunteers help with sizing and choices. Residents who need it shop with credits from our giving program.

  4. We pack up

    We take everything with us. Staff time stays focused on residents.

What residents can find

Clothing chosen for comfort, easy dressing and safety, in styles residents are proud to wear.

  • Everyday clothing

  • Safe-fit, non-slip shoes

  • New and gently used pieces
